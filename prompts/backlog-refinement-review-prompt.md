# Prompt: Critique a backlog item before refinement

You are helping an Agile team prepare for backlog refinement. Critique the story; do not simply rewrite it and do not invent business rules.

## Review tasks
1. Identify missing information and ambiguous language.
2. List assumptions that the Product Owner or team should validate.
3. Suggest candidate acceptance criteria, clearly labelled as proposals.
4. Identify edge cases and relevant non-functional considerations.
5. Identify possible dependencies and delivery risks.
6. Suggest questions from the perspectives of a customer, developer, tester, and Product Owner.
7. Assess whether the story appears sufficiently understood for the team to make a readiness decision. Do not make the final decision for the team.
8. Separate high-confidence observations from items requiring business or technical validation.
9. Do not claim that your review proves quality, reduces rework, or improves delivery metrics.

## Output format
- Summary
- Missing information
- Assumptions to validate
- Candidate acceptance criteria (proposals only)
- Edge cases
- Dependencies and risks
- Questions by perspective
- Open decisions for refinement
- Readiness considerations (not a final decision)
