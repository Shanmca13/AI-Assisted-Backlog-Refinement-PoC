# Human review log

Use one row per AI suggestion. This log separates AI-generated suggestions from team decisions.

| AI suggestion | Decision (accept / reject / validate) | Why | Owner / role | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example: clarify reset-link expiry | Validate | Business/security policy not provided | Product Owner + security reviewer | Confirm policy before finalizing criteria |
| Example: add rate limiting | Validate | Security control needs technical review | Developer / security reviewer | Check existing controls |

## Final refinement outcome
- Story revised?:
- Acceptance criteria agreed?:
- Dependencies confirmed?:
- Remaining questions:
- Team's readiness decision:
- Who participated in the decision?:

Do not mark a suggestion as accepted solely because AI generated it. Record the team's actual decision.
